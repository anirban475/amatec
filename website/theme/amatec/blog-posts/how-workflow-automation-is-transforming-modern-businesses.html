What Is Workflow Automation?

Workflow automation uses technology to create a streamlined, rules-based process that handles routine work automatically. From CRM updates and task assignments to follow-up communications and data syncing across tools—automation enables you to work smarter, not harder.

Whether you’re sending welcome emails to new leads, assigning service tickets, or updating your database across tools, automation eliminates the need for manual input and reduces costly errors.
