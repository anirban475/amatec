What is a Make.com Scenario?

A scenario is a visual automation workflow that connects two or more apps to perform a chain of actions—known as modules. It starts with a trigger (e.g., a new form submission) and proceeds through steps you define (e.g., send an email, update CRM, log data in a sheet).
