Step-by-Step Client Onboarding Automation Using Zoho CRM + Make.com

Below is a proven onboarding workflow used by high-performing UK agencies.

Step 1: Deal Closed → Client Record Created (Zoho CRM)

Once a deal is marked as “Won” in Zoho CRM:

  • a client record is automatically created
  • deal details are captured (services, pricing, timelines)
  • onboarding status is triggered

This ensures no client slips through after sales handover.

Step 3: Task & Project Setup Across Tools

Make.com automatically creates:

  • onboarding tasks in Zoho Projects / ClickUp / Monday.com
  • internal checklists for strategy, creatives, ads, SEO, or content

This removes manual setup and ensures every team follows the same process.

Step 4: Document Collection & Approvals

Automation sends requests for:

  • brand assets
  • access credentials
  • contracts
  • NDAs
  • payment confirmations

Once documents are uploaded or approved, Zoho CRM updates automatically no chasing required.

Step 5: Internal Team Notifications

Account managers, media buyers, designers, and SEO/content teams are all notified via Slack, Teams, or email alerts. Without meetings or follow-ups, everyone is aware when a client is prepared to go live.

Step 6: Client Status Tracking & Visibility

Zoho CRM dashboards show:

  • onboarding stage
  • pending actions
  • client readiness
  • bottlenecks

This visibility helps agency leaders manage workload and timelines confidently.
