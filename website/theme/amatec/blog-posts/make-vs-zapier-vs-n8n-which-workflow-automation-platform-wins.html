In today’s fast-paced business environment, workflow automation is essential—not optional. With repetitive tasks eating into productivity, platforms like Make (formerly Integromat), Zapier, and n8n have become go-to solutions. But when it comes to Make vs Zapier vs n8n, which one truly fits your B2B automation needs? Let’s compare their features, flexibility, and pricing.

Ease of Use

  • Zapier offers a completely no-code, user-friendly interface, ideal for marketing teams, sales reps, and non-technical users. Setting up automation (“Zaps”) is quick and requires no programming.
  • Make balances visual simplicity with powerful customization. Its drag-and-drop scenario builder supports complex logic while remaining accessible to business users.
  • n8n is best suited for developers and technical users. It’s a low-code automation tool with custom scripting capabilities, perfect for backend automation and system integration tasks.

Flexibility and Power

When comparing Make vs Zapier vs n8n in terms of flexibility:

  • Make excels in building multi-step workflows with advanced conditions, iterations, and robust error handling.
  • Zapier is ideal for quick automations between popular SaaS apps but lacks native support for loops and complex logic.
  • n8n stands out as the most powerful and developer-centric platform, with support for JavaScript functions, webhooks, and custom node creation.

Integration Ecosystem

  • Zapier leads the pack with 6,000+ app integrations, making it the most versatile for mainstream software.
  • Make supports 1,500+ apps and enables more advanced data transformation and logic control.
  • n8n provides 400+ native integrations, but shines with custom API connections and self-hosted capabilities for maximum control.

Pricing

  • Zapier can become costly at scale, with limits on tasks and feature access in lower-tier plans.
  • Make provides better value for complex workflows with its flexible and affordable plans.
  • n8n is completely open-source and free to self-host, making it ideal for developers and startups looking to scale without high costs.

Final Verdict

In the battle of Make vs Zapier vs n8n:

  • Choose Zapier for ease of use and quick integrations without coding.
  • Choose Make for visually rich, budget-conscious workflows with advanced logic.
  • Choose n8n for full control, custom integrations, and developer-driven automation.
