What is Workflow Automation?

Workflow automation is the process of using software to automate routine tasks, such as sending emails, updating databases, managing leads, or approving requests. Instead of spending hours on manual steps, your workflows run seamlessly in the background—accurately and efficiently.

Why Should You Automate?

Whether you’re a startup or a large enterprise, workflow automation can:

  • Save time by reducing manual work.
  • Improve accuracy by minimizing human error.
  • Boost productivity by freeing up your team for higher-value tasks.
  • Ensure consistency with standardized processes.
